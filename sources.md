## 8. Notes for documentary film use
- All visuals should be sourced from open-access museum collections, educational sites, archives, or official educational institutions.
- Avoid using modern reconstruction as if it were historical footage.
- Use only images that support the narration and align with historical understanding.
- Prefer museum, archive, and university sources over random web sources.
