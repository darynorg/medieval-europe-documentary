# Editing and production guide

## 1. Video format
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or 3840x2160
- Frame rate: 24fps or 25fps
- Color grade: warm ochre, dark brown, gold, muted green and gray tones
- Fonts: serif, classical, academic

## 2. Narrative timing
- Intro title: 30-40 seconds
- Section 1: 1 min 30 sec
- Section 2: 1 min 40 sec
- Section 3: 1 min 45 sec
- Section 4: 1 min 35 sec
- Section 5: 1 min 35 sec
- Section 6: 1 min 40 sec
- Conclusion: 40 sec
- Final title: 30 sec
- Total: about 11 minutes

## 3. Audio style
- Narration in Kazakh, professional and calm
- Music: atmospheric and slow, no strong drums, no shifting bass
- Volume: voice +70%, music +30% lower than narration
- Include soft ambient sounds if necessary: choir, bells, wind, cathedral atmosphere

## 4. Recommended soundtrack direction
- Classical orchestral, medieval-inspired ambient, sacred choral textures
- Avoid heavy modern synth or pop rhythms
- Keep music as background support, not a spectacle in itself

## 5. Editing principles
- Use slow cinematic transitions
- Combine architecture, manuscripts, map animations, and closeups
- Do not overload each section with text
- Use each section to show one main concept clearly
- Intercut visuals with narration naturally

## 6. Visual set of media
- Historical architecture: cathedrals, churches, castles
- Manuscripts and old books
- Maps of medieval Europe
- Daily life: markets, workshops, dwellings
- Religious art: stained glass, murals, mosaics
- Literary references and universities

## 7. Suggested post-production workflow
1. Gather all historical media.
2. Sort visuals by section.
3. Build a rough cut with narration.
4. Add music and quiet transitions.
5. Adjust timing and pacing.
6. Add final title cards.
7. Export in 1080p and 4K versions.

## 8. Recommendation for color and atmosphere
- Gold and clay tones for warmth
- Dark grey and muted blue for depth
- Warm light at sunrise on stone architecture
- Use soft contrast to maintain historical respect

## 9. Export settings
- H.264 main for web
- ProRes 422 for master file
- Delivery: 1920x1080 24fps or 25fps

## 10. Final production note
The film should feel substantial, dignified, and thoughtful. It is not a sensational documentary; it is a cultural and academic film that invites the audience to understand medieval Europe as a period of brilliance, spirituality, creativity, and social organization.
